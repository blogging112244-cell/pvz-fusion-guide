
# PvZ Fusion Guide

A fan-made resource for Plants vs. Zombies Fusion players. This repository covers plant fusion combinations, gameplay tips, strategies, and useful information for exploring the game.

## About PvZ Fusion

Plants vs. Zombies Fusion is a fan-made project inspired by the Plants vs. Zombies series. Its fusion mechanics encourage players to experiment with different plant combinations and discover new ways to defend their garden.

## What This Guide Covers

- Plant fusion combinations and gameplay ideas
- Beginner tips and strategies
- Information about plants and their abilities
- Helpful resources for PvZ Fusion players
